Designed specifically for 15kg SmallSat buses, this technology solves the critical longevity bottlenecks caused by severe orbital environments—including atomic oxygen (AO) erosion, aggressive thermal cycling, and micrometeoroid/orbital debris (MMOD) impacts.

### 💼 Commercial & Financial ROI
* **Extended Constellation Lifespan:** Mitigates orbital degradation, drastically reducing satellite replacement frequency and capital expenditure (CapEx) for commercial operators.

* **Reduced Maintenance Overhead:** Self-healing nanoscale mechanisms reduce structural failure risks, yielding higher operational uptime and superior return on investment (ROI) for commercial satellite networks.
